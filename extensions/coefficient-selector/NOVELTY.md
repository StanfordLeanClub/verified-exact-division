# Contribution and prior-art boundary

The coefficient-selector novelty gate was completed before the earlier checker and benchmarking work. Its full record is retained in the separate selector investigation and remains in the local evidence bundle. It found antecedents for multiply/FMA constant division, rounding-boundary searches, and evaluation-aware coefficient synthesis. No exact match for the particular adjacent-pair selector and same-sign specialization was located; priority was not established.

This continuation adds an IEEE bridge for arbitrary supplied pairs, exact binary32 encoding/guard proofs, a SIMD lane-reduction and block/list proof, and a tighter sufficient threshold. The bridge makes no assumption that the high coefficient is the nearest reciprocal. It can be reused with other checker-certified pairs once its explicit range bounds are discharged. The fixed `/3` instance is the complete all-encoding application; arbitrary-pair IEEE setup and native generation are not claimed to be complete.

The threshold refinement solves the existing bound `T*abs(l) >= minNormal` more tightly. Eliminating the cold helper's redundant scan follows from the proved equality of its finite-tiny mask with the fast path's mask. These are ordinary bound tightening and specialization, not newly invented arithmetic algorithms. The strict decrease in flagged input encodings is proved; elapsed-time benefits require measurement.

Relevant primary sources remain [BMR (2004)](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/jean-michel.muller/117197.pdf), [Brisebarre and Muller (2007)](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/jean-michel.muller/Journal_MultFMAC_Final.pdf), [Boldo et al. (2015), verified floating-point compilation](https://jhjourdan.mketjh.fr/pdf/boldo2015verified.pdf), and [RLibm-32 (2021)](https://people.cs.rutgers.edu/~santosh.nagarakatte/papers/rlibm32-pldi-2021-preprint.pdf). An additional search on 12 November 2026 for same-sign constant division, FMA, scaling and subnormal handling found no reason to change the earlier cautious priority assessment. Scaling and final rounding need their own proofs; naively scaling a rounded result back down can double-round and is not adopted here.

The defensible contribution is the checked coefficient freedom, the useful instance and measured consequences, and the additional proof coverage. Neither this search nor a successful proof establishes that the overall idea is novel.
